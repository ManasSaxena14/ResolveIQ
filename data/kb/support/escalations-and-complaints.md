# Escalations and Complaints

## Asking for a supervisor

If you are not satisfied with the answer from a support agent, you can ask for the case to be escalated to a supervisor. A supervisor reviews the case and calls or emails you back within 1 business day.

## Formal complaints

If the issue is still not resolved after escalation, you can submit a formal complaint through the Complaints form on the Help Center. The Customer Relations team sends a written response within 5 business days.

## What to include

- Your order number and case number
- A short description of the problem and what you have already tried
- The outcome you are asking for

## Case numbers

Every conversation with support creates a case number that starts with CS-. Quote it when you follow up so you do not have to explain the issue again.
