# Contacting Altura Support

## Channels and hours

| Channel | Availability | Expected response |
|---|---|---|
| Live chat | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week | A few minutes |
| Phone | Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET, Saturday and Sunday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. ET | Varies by demand |
| Email (support@altura.example) | Any time | Within 24 hours |

Altura Plus members can use the priority phone line, which has shorter waiting times.

## Before you contact us

Have your order number ready. It starts with ALT- and is in your confirmation email and under Account > Orders.

## Languages

Support is available in English and Spanish on all channels.

## Your security

Altura Support will never ask for your password, a one-time code or your full card number.
