# Deleting Your Account and Data

## Requesting deletion

Go to Settings > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion starts with a 14-day grace period, during which you can sign in to cancel the request. After the grace period, personal data is deleted within 30 days.

Open orders, active returns and an active Altura Plus membership must be completed or cancelled before the account can be deleted.

## Data we must keep

Order and payment records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it. These records are not used for marketing.

## Exporting your data

Before deleting your account you can download a copy of your data from Settings > Privacy > Export data. The export is provided as a JSON file and is ready within 48 hours. A download link is emailed to you.

## Gift card balances

Any remaining gift card balance linked to the account is lost when the account is deleted. Use the balance first or ask support to move it to a physical card.
