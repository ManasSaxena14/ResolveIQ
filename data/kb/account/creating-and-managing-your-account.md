# Creating and Managing Your Account

## Creating an account

You can create an Altura account with an email address and password. Each email address can be linked to only one account.

## Changing your email address

Go to Settings > Profile and enter the new email address. We send a verification link to the new address. The link is valid for 24 hours, and the change takes effect only after you click it.

## Saved addresses and cards

You can save up to 10 shipping addresses and 5 payment cards. Card details are stored by our payment processor and Altura never sees the full card number.

## Merging accounts

Two accounts cannot be merged. Order history stays with the account that placed the order.

## Communication preferences

Order and account emails are always sent. Marketing emails can be turned off at any time in Settings > Notifications or with the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
