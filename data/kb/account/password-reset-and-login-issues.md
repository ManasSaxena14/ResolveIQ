# Password Reset and Login Issues

## Resetting your password

Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter your account email. The password reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.

## Reset email not arriving

Check your spam or junk folder and wait up to 10 minutes. Make sure you entered the email address linked to your account. For security reasons, the page does not confirm whether an account exists for the email you entered.

## Account locked

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. You can wait for the lock to expire or reset your password to unlock it immediately.

## Signing out of all devices

If you think someone else has access to your account, reset your password and choose Sign out of all devices in Settings > Security.
