# Two-Factor Authentication

## Turning on two-factor authentication

Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication. You can receive codes through an authenticator app or by text message (SMS). An authenticator app is recommended because it works without mobile signal.

## Backup codes

When you turn on two-factor authentication, Altura gives you 10 single-use backup codes. Store them somewhere safe. Each code can be used once if you cannot access your phone.

## Lost phone or authenticator

If you lose access to your second factor and have no backup codes, contact Altura Support to recover the account. For your security we verify your identity first, which takes up to 2 business days. Support agents never ask for your password or a full card number during this process.

## Turning it off

Two-factor authentication can be turned off in Settings > Security after confirming your password and a current code.
