Privacy and Data Use

What we collect

Altura collects the information needed to process orders and support requests: your name, contact details, shipping and billing addresses, order history and conversations with support.

How we use your data

We use your data to deliver orders, provide support, prevent fraud and, if you agree, send marketing emails. Altura does not sell personal data to third parties.

Cookies

Essential cookies are always active because the store cannot work without them. Analytics and advertising cookies are optional and can be turned off at any time from the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Your rights

  • Access and export your data from Settings > Privacy.
  • Correct inaccurate information in your profile.
  • Delete your account and personal data.
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time.

Support conversations

Chat and email conversations with support are kept for 2 years to help resolve follow-up questions and to train support staff, then deleted.
