# Cancelling Altura Plus

## How to cancel

You can cancel Altura Plus at any time in Settings > Membership > Cancel membership. You receive a confirmation email once the cancellation is complete.

## Monthly plans

When you cancel a monthly plan, your benefits continue until the end of the current billing period. Partial months are not refunded.

## Annual plans

If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days of being charged and have not used any membership benefits, you receive a full refund. After 14 days, or if benefits have been used, the plan is not refunded and benefits continue until the end of the membership year.

## Cancelling during the free trial

If you cancel during the 30-day free trial, you are not charged and the trial benefits end immediately.

## Orders placed while a member

Returns for orders placed while you were a member keep the 60-day return window and free return shipping, even after the membership ends.
