# Altura Plus Membership

## Price

Altura Plus costs $9.99 per month or $99 per year.

## Benefits

- Free Express shipping on every order, with no minimum order value
- Extended 60-day return window
- Free return shipping on all returns
- Early access to seasonal sales
- A priority phone line with shorter waiting times

## Free trial

New members can try Altura Plus free for 30 days. The free trial is available once per customer. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the plan you selected is charged automatically.

## Sharing a membership

A membership belongs to one account and cannot be shared with other accounts or households.

## Changing between monthly and annual plans

You can switch plans in Settings > Membership. The new plan starts at the end of the current billing period.
