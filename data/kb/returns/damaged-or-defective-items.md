# Damaged or Defective Items

## Items damaged in shipping

Report shipping damage within 7 days of delivery. Contact Altura Support with your order number and photos of the item and the outer box. Keep the packaging until the claim is closed, because the carrier may ask to inspect it.

## Defective items within the return window

If an item stops working or does not work out of the box within the return window, you can choose a free replacement or a full refund. Return shipping is free and no restocking fee applies.

## Defects after the return window

After the return window has ended, defects are handled under the product warranty. See the Warranty Coverage article for how to make a claim.

## Wrong item received

If you received a different item from the one you ordered, contact support within 14 days of delivery. We send the correct item at no cost and provide a prepaid label for the wrong one.
