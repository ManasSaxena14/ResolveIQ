# Return Policy

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Altura Plus members have an extended return window of 60 days.

Items must be returned with all accessories, manuals and original packaging. Devices must be signed out of any personal accounts, such as cloud or find-my-device services, before they are sent back.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Opened in-ear headphones and earbuds, for hygiene reasons, unless they are defective
- Digital downloads and software license keys once revealed
- Gift cards

## Restocking fee

There is no restocking fee on most items. Opened drones and cameras carry a 10 percent restocking fee unless they are defective or arrived damaged.

## Holiday returns

Items delivered between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.
