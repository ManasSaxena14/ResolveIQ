# Refund Timelines

## When the refund is issued

Altura issues the refund within 3 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse. You receive an email when the refund has been issued.

## When you will see the money

After the refund is issued, the time until it appears depends on the payment method:

| Payment method | Time to appear |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 10 business days |
| PayPal | 1 to 3 business days |
| Altura gift card | Immediately, as store credit |
| Pay Later installments | Remaining installments are cancelled within 3 business days |

## What is refunded

The full item price is refunded, minus any return label fee or restocking fee that applies. Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the return is because of an error by Altura, such as a wrong or defective item.

## Refund to a different method

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. They cannot be redirected to another card or bank account.
