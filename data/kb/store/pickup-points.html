Collecting an Order from a Pickup Point

Altura is an online-only retailer and does not have physical stores. Instead, many orders can be collected free of charge from partner pickup points.

Ready times

Orders placed before 3 p.m. on a business day are ready for collection the next business day. You receive an email and a text message with a collection code when the order is ready.

How long we hold your order

Orders are held at the pickup point for 7 days. Uncollected orders are returned to Altura and refunded in full, including any shipping charge.

What to bring

Bring the collection code and a government-issued photo ID that matches the name on the order. Someone else can collect for you if you add their name as an alternate collector at checkout.

Items not available for pickup

Freight items, such as televisions over 65 inches, cannot be sent to pickup points.
