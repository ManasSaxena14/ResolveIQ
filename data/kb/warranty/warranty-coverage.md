# Warranty Coverage

## Manufacturer warranty

Every product sold by Altura includes the manufacturer's warranty, which is usually 1 year from the delivery date. The exact length is listed on the product page.

## First 30 days

If a product develops a fault within 30 days of delivery, Altura replaces it directly so you do not have to deal with the manufacturer. After 30 days, warranty claims are handled by the manufacturer. Altura Support can give you the manufacturer's contact details and your proof of purchase.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Accidental damage such as drops and cracked screens
- Liquid or water damage
- Damage caused by repairs from unauthorized service providers
- Normal wear, such as scratches and battery capacity loss over time

Accidental damage is covered only by an Altura Care protection plan.

## Proof of purchase

Your invoice serves as proof of purchase. You can download it at any time under Account > Orders.
