# Altura Care Protection Plans

## What Altura Care is

Altura Care is an optional protection plan for laptops, phones, tablets and headphones. It is available for 2 or 3 years from the device purchase date.

## When you can buy it

Altura Care can be added at checkout or within 60 days of the device purchase.

## What it covers

- Mechanical and electrical failures after the manufacturer warranty ends
- Accidental damage, including drops and liquid spills, for up to 2 claims per year
- Battery replacement when capacity falls below 80 percent

Each accidental damage claim has a $49 service fee. Mechanical failures have no service fee.

## Cancelling a plan

You can cancel Altura Care within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. After 30 days, you receive a prorated refund for the unused time, minus the value of any claims already paid.

## Transferring a plan

If you sell or give away the device, the plan can be transferred to the new owner by contacting Altura Support.
