DEVICE SETUP AND TROUBLESHOOTING

General first steps

Before contacting support about a device problem, try these steps in order:
1. Restart the device completely, not just the screen.
2. Install all available software and firmware updates.
3. Check cables, chargers and power outlets with another device.
4. Remove recently installed apps or accessories.

Many common problems, such as freezing, poor Wi-Fi connections and battery drain, are fixed by these steps.

Smart home hub pairing

Altura smart home hubs pair with devices using the Altura Home app. Keep the device within 3 meters of the hub during pairing. If pairing fails, hold the reset button on the device for 10 seconds until the light flashes blue, then try again. A hub supports up to 64 connected devices.

Laptop battery not charging

Use the original charger, because many laptops do not charge with low-power phone chargers. If the battery still does not charge, run the built-in battery health check from the manufacturer's support app.

Factory reset

A factory reset erases all data. Back up your data first and sign out of all accounts before resetting a device you plan to return or trade in.
