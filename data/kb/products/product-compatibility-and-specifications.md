# Product Compatibility and Specifications

## Where to find specifications

Each product page has a Specifications tab with dimensions, weight, ports, battery life and compatibility. The information comes from the manufacturer and is updated when they publish changes.

## Charger and accessory compatibility

Altura lists compatible accessories under Works with on each product page. Accessories listed there have been confirmed to work with the product. Using uncertified chargers can slow down charging and may void the manufacturer warranty.

## Regional versions

All phones sold by Altura are unlocked US versions and work with all major US carriers. Some features, such as certain 5G bands, may not work outside the United States.

## Questions before buying

If the product page does not answer your question, use the Ask a question section on the page or contact Altura Support through live chat. Support can confirm compatibility, but cannot give advice about products Altura does not sell.
