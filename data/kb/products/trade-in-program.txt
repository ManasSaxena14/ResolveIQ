TRADE-IN PROGRAM

Eligible devices

Altura accepts trade-ins of phones, tablets, laptops and smartwatches released in the last 6 years. Devices must power on and must not be reported lost or stolen.

Getting a quote

Answer a few questions about the device's condition on the Trade-In page to get an instant quote. The quote is valid for 14 days.

Sending the device

After you accept the quote, Altura emails a free prepaid shipping label. A protective shipping kit can be requested for phones and tablets at no cost. Remove SIM and memory cards, sign out of all accounts and perform a factory reset before sending the device.

Payment

Trade-in value is paid as an Altura gift card within 5 business days of the device being inspected. Trade-in value cannot be paid in cash.

Revised quotes

If the device's condition does not match your answers, Altura sends a revised quote. You can accept it, or decline it and have the device returned to you free of charge.
