# Shipping Options and Costs

Altura ships to all 50 US states. Delivery times are counted in business days, Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4 to 6 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99 |
| Express | 2 business days | $14.99 |
| Next-day | Next business day | $24.99 |

## Next-day cut-off

Next-day orders must be placed before 2 p.m. local time, Monday to Friday. Orders placed after the cut-off or at the weekend are delivered on the second business day.

## Altura Plus members

Altura Plus members receive free Express shipping on every order, with no minimum order value.

## Restrictions

Next-day delivery is not available to Alaska, Hawaii or PO boxes. Large items such as televisions over 65 inches ship by freight and are delivered within 7 business days.
