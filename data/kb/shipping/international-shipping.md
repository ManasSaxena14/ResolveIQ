# International Shipping

## Countries we ship to

Outside the United States, Altura ships to Canada, the United Kingdom, all European Union countries and Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days.

## Duties and taxes

For the United Kingdom and the European Union, VAT is calculated and collected at checkout, so there is nothing more to pay on delivery. For Canada and Australia, import duties and taxes are paid by the customer to the carrier on delivery.

## Restrictions

- Standalone lithium batteries and power banks cannot be shipped internationally.
- PO boxes are not accepted for international orders.
- Express and Next-day options are only available within the United States.

## Refused international deliveries

If an international package is refused because of duties, the cost of return shipping is deducted from the refund.
