# Delayed or Lost Packages

## Tracking has not updated

Carriers sometimes scan packages only at major hubs. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, contact Altura Support with your order number and we will open a trace with the carrier.

## Lost packages

A package is treated as lost when it has not been delivered 10 business days after the estimated delivery date. Once a lost package claim is approved, you can choose between a free replacement and a full refund.

## Marked as delivered but not received

If tracking shows Delivered but you cannot find the package:

1. Wait 48 hours, because carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered early.
2. Check with neighbors, building reception and any safe place the carrier may have used.
3. If it is still missing, file a claim with Altura Support within 30 days of the delivery date.

Claims filed more than 30 days after the delivery date cannot be accepted.
