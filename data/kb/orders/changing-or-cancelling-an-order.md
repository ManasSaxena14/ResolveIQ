# Changing or Cancelling an Order

## Free cancellation window

You can cancel an order at no cost within 1 hour of placing it, or at any time while its status is still Received. Go to Account > Orders, select the order and choose Cancel order. The payment authorization is released immediately, although your bank may take a few days to remove the pending amount.

## Changing the shipping address

The shipping address can be edited while the order status is Received. Once the order is Processing, the address is locked because the shipping label has already been created.

## After the order has shipped

Shipped orders cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery, in which case the package returns to us and a refund is issued once it arrives, or you can accept it and start a normal return.

## Changing items

Items cannot be added to or removed from an existing order. Cancel the order within the free cancellation window and place a new one.
