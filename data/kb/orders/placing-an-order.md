# Placing an Order

Altura is an online electronics retailer selling laptops, phones, audio equipment and smart home devices.

## Checkout options

You can check out with an Altura account or as a guest. Guest orders can be tracked with the order number and the email address used at checkout, but they cannot be linked to an account later.

## Order confirmation

After checkout you receive an order confirmation email within 15 minutes. Every order number starts with ALT- followed by eight digits, for example ALT-20481937. If the email does not arrive within 15 minutes, check your spam folder and then confirm the order under Account > Orders.

## Quantity limits

To keep stock available for all customers, you can buy at most 5 units of the same product in one order. Orders that exceed this limit are automatically reduced to 5 units and you are only charged for the units that ship.

## When an order is accepted

Placing an order is an offer to buy. The order is accepted when it ships. Until then, Altura may cancel an order because of a pricing error or a failed payment check, and any charge is fully reversed.
