# Order Status and Tracking

## Order statuses

Each order moves through these statuses:

1. Received: we have your order and payment authorization.
2. Processing: the warehouse is picking and packing your items.
3. Shipped: the package has left the warehouse.
4. Out for delivery: the carrier expects to deliver it today.
5. Delivered: the carrier has confirmed delivery.

## Tracking your package

When an order ships, we email a tracking link. You can also find it under Account > Orders > Track package. Guest customers can use the Track Order page with the order number and checkout email.

Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after you receive the shipping email. This is normal and does not mean the package is lost.

## Orders with several packages

Large orders may ship in more than one package from different warehouses. Each package has its own tracking number, and all of them are listed on the order page.
