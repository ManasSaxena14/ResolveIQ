# Invoices and Receipts

## Downloading an invoice

A PDF invoice is available for every shipped order under Account > Orders > Download invoice. Guest customers receive the invoice as an attachment to the shipping confirmation email.

## Business invoices

To get an invoice with your company name and VAT or tax ID, add these details in the Business details section at checkout before you place the order.

## Correcting invoice details

Invoice details such as the company name or billing address can be corrected within 30 days of the invoice date by contacting Altura Support. After 30 days, invoices cannot be changed because they have been filed for tax reporting.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated from the shipping address and shown as a separate line on the invoice. Altura does not process sales tax exemption certificates for individual customers.
