# Double Charges and Pending Authorizations

## Pending authorizations

When you place an order, Altura places a temporary authorization hold on your card to confirm the funds are available. The actual charge is taken when the order ships. If you edit or cancel an order, the original hold may still be visible for a while. Pending holds drop off automatically within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

A pending hold next to a completed charge for the same amount is not a double charge.

## Real duplicate charges

If your statement shows two completed charges for the same order, contact Altura Support with:

- the order number
- the last four digits of the card
- the dates and amounts of both charges

Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded within 5 business days.

## Charged for a cancelled order

If an order was cancelled before it shipped, no charge is taken. Only the authorization hold may remain visible until it expires.
