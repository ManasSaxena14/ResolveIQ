# Price Adjustments

## Our price-drop promise

If Altura lowers the price of an item within 14 days of its delivery, you can request a one-time price adjustment for the difference. Contact Altura Support with your order number and a link to the product page.

The difference is refunded to the original payment method.

## What is not eligible

Price adjustments are not available for:

- prices offered by other retailers
- flash sales and limited-time deals that last less than 24 hours
- clearance and open-box items
- items bought with a promo code

## Number of adjustments

Each item in an order can receive only one price adjustment, even if the price drops again later.
