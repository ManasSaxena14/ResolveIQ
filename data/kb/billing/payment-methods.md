# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods

Altura accepts:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover credit and debit cards
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay
- Altura gift cards

Cash on delivery, checks and bank transfers are not accepted.

## Pay Later installments

Orders between $100 and $3,000 can be paid in 4 interest-free installments, charged every two weeks. The first installment is charged when the order ships. Approval is decided at checkout and does not affect your credit score.

## Splitting a payment

An order can be paid with up to two gift cards plus one other payment method. It is not possible to split one order across two credit cards.

## Billing address

The billing address must match the address registered with your card issuer. A mismatch is the most common reason a payment is declined.
