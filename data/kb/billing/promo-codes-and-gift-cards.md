# Promo Codes and Gift Cards

## Promo codes

- Only one promo code can be used per order.
- Promo codes cannot be combined with a price adjustment on the same item.
- A promo code cannot be added after the order has been placed.
- If you return part of an order, the discount is shared proportionally across the items, so the refund for each item reflects the discount it received.

## Gift cards

Altura gift cards are available from $10 to $500. They never expire and have no fees.

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law. They cannot be used to buy other gift cards.

## Lost or stolen gift cards

A lost or stolen gift card can only be replaced if you can provide the original purchase receipt and the remaining balance has not been used.

## Checking a balance

You can check a gift card balance at checkout or on the Gift Cards page by entering the card number and PIN.
